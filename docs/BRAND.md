# Brand

## Name

**writ.**

A writ is a formal authorization to act, and the written record that authority
was granted. That is the product: every AI-agent action is authorized before it
runs and recorded afterwards.

## Backronym

**WRIT = Warranted Runtime for Intelligent Tools**

- **Warranted** — the action is authorized, justified, and attributable.
- **Runtime** — enforcement happens at the moment the tool call would run, not
  later in a dashboard.
- **Intelligent Tools** — AI agents, MCP tools, shell commands, SDK tools, and
  the systems those agents reach.

Use the acronym as a memorable expansion, not as a replacement for the name.
The public name remains short and command-friendly: `writ`.

## Taglines

- **Nothing runs without a writ.**
- Govern and prove every tool call your AI agent makes.
- One policy file. One tamper-evident ledger. Any agent.

## Voice

Writ sounds like infrastructure, not a hall monitor. Say exactly what it does,
what it records, and what it does not claim to solve.
